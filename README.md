Jetpack Joe:GOLD RUSH

# THE GAME:

Jetpack Joe: Gold Rush is a retro-style 2D endless arcade game in unity.

In this game you play as a tiny miner equipped with a jetpact and explore a dangerous underground cave,as
you use your jetpack to fly through tons of deadly spikes.
A single mishap and GAME OVER,the longer you survive,the high your score becomes.

Grab gold in deadly locations,but make sure you know which ones are too risky to grab and which ones are safe,navigate your way through the endless obsticles,and survive as long as you can!

CHALLENGES I FACED WHILE MAKING THE GAME:
When making this game, I had very very little coding experience, learning unity from scratch and C# code.
I struggled in joining scripts together and constantly had to deal with errors after every step.

The biggest challenge i faced was learning how to properly operate the code for numerous scripts,
making the gold spawn in precise and risky locations for the player to grab.


GAMEPLAY-->

The player can control Jetpack Joe using the keyboard.
Jetpack joe will automatically move forward though the cave,the main goal is to rack up as much of a top score as possible
Passing a spike increases the score points by 1.
Collecting a gold chunk increases the score by 5 points

If the player hits the ceiling,ground,or a spike, the game will be over and you must restart.
The player must strategize which gold chunks are too risky to take and which are possible and efficient to capture.


CONTROLS-->
1.Space--> Single press = Use jeckpack/fly upwards
2.Collect points------> Fly in between spikes
3.Collect gold--------> Pass through the gold with Jetpack Joe
4.To restart----------> Click the restart button in the game over screen
5.Move forward--------> Automatic
